  • 47 sites remaining from 2024 data
  • 2025 descriptions are more complete and better suited for newer team members

47.5.2 Site Numbering System

  • 2025 sites starting fresh at numbering sequence 2025MNxxx001, 002, 003…
  • Caution needed when entering data to ensure correct year assignment
  • Survey123 auto-populates current year, requiring manual adjustment for 2024 data

47.5.3 Data Entry Technical Notes (from Patty)

Special character restrictions in NASIS field notes text box: - No dashes, carets (^), or ampersands (&) - Write out “caret” instead of using ^ symbol - Write “and” instead of & symbol - Don’t specify HCl type in effervescence descriptions - Stick to basic effervescence terms (slight, strong, etc.)

47.5.4 Concept Assignment

  • Team members should assign soil concepts during field work
  • Concept information to be recorded in pedon tracker spreadsheet
  • Nora will print hard copy of concept list for field reference
