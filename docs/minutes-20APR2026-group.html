69.2 Green Spirit Community Garden — Lead Sampling (Ava, Nic)

  • pXRF Green Spirit Community Garden in St. Paul returned lead readings of 4,000–7,000 ppm in a horizon where an old metal plant hanger was removed; the site was formerly a neighborhood and rail yard
  • The high-lead samples came from a non-food-producing area of the garden and were not at the surface
  • Ava and Kasia have a personal plot at Green Spirit and are connected with the site; Ava has been in contact with Erin, who appears to coordinate garden activities
  • Nic agreed the group should invest time in additional screening of actively managed, food-producing areas to give a more comprehensive and representative assessment to the garden
  • Ava plans to collect samples from a few depths within her plot on Wednesday and attend a group garden event Saturday at 9:30 to speak with community members
  • Ava will also follow up with Adam regarding the original sampling contact
  • For follow-up communications with the garden, Nic suggested framing it as wanting to screen food-producing areas to be more comprehensive in reporting
  • Focus will be on lead only; pH, organic matter, and other measurements will not be a concern for this follow-up

69.3 Tomorrow’s Meeting with Randy — Wetness Criteria Discussion (Patty, Nora, Nic)

  • A meeting with Randy is scheduled for tomorrow at 10:30
  • Patty received an email from Randy last week regarding questions on Adam’s key, specifically around how to categorize wetness criteria
  • The open question is whether to use all standard drainage classes (very poorly drained through well-drained) or to lump some categories — e.g., poorly drained and somewhat poorly drained — into a single concept
  • A specific sticking point is where to place “oxyaquic” — whether it aligns more with moderately well-drained or well-drained
  • The group agreed to discuss this collectively tomorrow; Joe will be present, and Nic confirmed he will also attend
  • Adam asked that the group discuss and bring him into the loop after he returns
